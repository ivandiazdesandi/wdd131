Learn the Foundations

A quick, beginner-friendly guide to FOREX and Crypto so you can use the tools with confidence.

FOREX Basics

The foreign exchange (FOREX) market trades currencies in pairs (e.g., EUR/USD). The first currency is the base, the second is the quote. If EUR/USD = 1.0800, one euro costs 1.08 US dollars.

Key Terms

Pip
Smallest price move (usually 0.0001). Used to measure distance and profit/loss.
Spread
Difference between bid and ask; a trading cost.
Lot Size
Trade volume (micro 0.01, mini 0.10, standard 1.00).
Session
Active market hours (London, New York, Tokyo, Sydney).

How Profit/Loss Works

P/L depends on: distance in pips, lot size, and the pair’s pip value. Our Pip & P/L calculator does this for you.

Crypto Basics

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets that trade 24/7 and can be highly volatile. You’ll typically store assets in a wallet and trade on an exchange using spot or derivatives.

Key Concepts

  • Coins vs. Tokens: Coins run on their own chain (e.g., BTC); tokens live on another chain (e.g., ERC-20 on Ethereum).
  • Wallets: Custodial (held by an exchange) or non-custodial (you hold the keys).
  • Fees & Slippage: Know your costs and expected fill.

Risk Snapshot

Crypto moves fast. Manage position size, use stop-losses, and avoid over-leverage. Use our simple Crypto P/L tool to preview outcomes.
