Site Plan: TradeWise Markets

Site Name

TradeWise Markets

This name reflects a platform offering smart insights into trading both FOREX and cryptocurrency markets.

Site Purpose

The site provides educational content on FOREX and crypto trading fundamentals, interactive calculators, market analysis, and a personalized watchlist feature.

Scenarios

  • How do I calculate my profit and loss for a currency trade?
  • Which crypto assets have the highest historical volatility?

Color Schema

  • #1a237e – Used for headings and navigation background
  • #ffb300 – Used for buttons and highlights

Typography

  • Playfair Display – Used for headings (h1, h2, h3)
  • Work Sans – Used for body text, lists, and paragraph content
